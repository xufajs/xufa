Overview Reference

Reference

Forms of a site, as django.forms: fields that clean what a body gives (texts, dates, choices, objects of a model, files), forms with their errors by field and their HTML, and forms of models of @xufa/orm (ModelForm) checked by the model, with no dependencies. Every declaration of its TypeScript declarations (packages/forms/index.d.ts or index.d.cts), as they are written, with their comments: made from them each time the docs are built. How to use them is in the overview.
