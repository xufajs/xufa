Overview Reference

Reference

Views of classes, as Django's generic views: View, TemplateView, RedirectView, ListView and DetailView of models of @xufa/orm, and FormView, CreateView, UpdateView and DeleteView with the forms of @xufa/forms. Every declaration of its TypeScript declarations (packages/views/index.d.ts or index.d.cts), as they are written, with their comments: made from them each time the docs are built. How to use them is in the overview.
