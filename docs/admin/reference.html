Overview Reference

Reference

The admin of the models of @xufa/orm, as Django's, with no dependencies to install: a React page (built in) with lists, search, filters, order and pages, forms from the fields with their validation, jobs and pipeline runs, roles and tenants, as a plugin of @xufa/http. Every declaration of its TypeScript declarations (packages/admin/index.d.ts or index.d.cts), as they are written, with their comments: made from them each time the docs are built. How to use them is in the overview.

AdminModelOptions

export interface AdminModelOptions { ... }

The options of a model in the admin: its list, what it searches and filters by, its fields only read.

MemberType
label?string

Its name as a collection (the sidebar, its list): its meta.labelPlural with a capital ('Book instances').

singular?string

Its name for one object ('Add book instance'): its meta.label.

list?Array<string | AdminColumn>

The columns of its list (the first six fields that are not text nor json), as Django's list_display: fields, paths across foreign keys ('book__title', ordered by it), methods or getters of the model ('displayGenre', async too; its header: the label of the function), many-to-many fields, or columns of their own.

editable?string[]

Fields of its list changed in its rows, booleans and choices, each saved as it changes (Django's list_editable).

fields?Array<string | string[]>

The fields of its form and their order (Django's fields): names, or lists of names on one line.

fieldsets?Array< [string | null, { fields: Array<string | string[]>; description?: string; collapse?: boolean; classes?: string[] }] >

The fields of its form in groups (Django's fieldsets): [title or null, { fields, description, collapse }].

search?string[]

The fields its search looks in (its string fields).

filters?string[]

The fields it filters by (booleans, choices and foreign keys).

readOnly?string[]

Fields shown but not changed in its forms (besides keys, automatic dates and given fields).

ordering?string[]

Its default order (that of the model).

readOnlyModel?boolean

Only read: no creates, updates nor deletes.

inlines?Array<string | AdminInline> | false

Its related lists (the objects of other models of the admin that point to it), by relation name (bookSet): all by default, none with false. { relation, fields, extra } edits them in rows (Django's TabularInline).

actions?Record<string, AdminActionRun | AdminAction>

Its actions on the objects selected in its list (as Django's), by name: run, or { run, label, permission, confirm, danger }.
